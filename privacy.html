Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours.

The short version: my apps don't collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Who I am

This policy covers the apps published by Milan Peter, an independent developer ("I", "me"), and this website. If you have any question about it, email milan.peter.dev@icloud.com.

What I collect

Nothing. I don't run servers for my apps, and the apps don't send me any information about you or how you use them. I can't see your data, so I can't lose it, sell it or hand it over.

If you email me, I receive your email address and what you write, and I use it only to answer you. I don't add you to any list.

Crash reports

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They don't identify you. You can turn this off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

OneClickTranslate

  • Web pages. When you click the button, the extension reads the text of the current page so it can be translated. Translation is done on your Mac by Apple's built-in Translation framework. The text is not sent to me or to any other service.
  • Language models. The first time you translate from a new language, macOS may download that language from Apple. This is handled by macOS under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Settings. Your chosen language and display options are stored only on your Mac.
  • Website access. Safari asks you which websites the extension may access. You can change this at any time in Safari › Settings › Extensions.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other services. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep technical logs, such as IP addresses, for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

My apps don't collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update it here and change the date at the top.
