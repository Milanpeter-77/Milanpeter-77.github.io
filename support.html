QuickTranslate

A Safari extension for the Mac that translates the page you're on with one click, on your Mac, without reloading it. QuickTranslate is coming soon to the Mac App Store — your feedback helps a lot.

How do I turn on the extension?

Open QuickTranslate and click Turn On in Safari…. In Safari's settings, tick QuickTranslate and allow it on the websites you want to translate. Its button then appears in Safari's toolbar.

How do I choose the language pages are translated into?

Open the QuickTranslate app and choose it under Translate pages into. The extension uses it on your next click.

Is there a keyboard shortcut?

Yes: ⌥⇧T (Option-Shift-T) translates the page, and pressing it again puts the original back, the same as clicking the button.

What do the letters on the toolbar button mean?
  • An outlined circle with a language (FR, DE…): the page's language; the page can be translated.
  • … (three dots): the page is being translated.
  • A filled circle with your language (EN…): the page is translated. Click again for the original.
  • A: the page's language isn't known yet or can't be translated, or you've turned the language off in the app (Show the language on the toolbar button).
How do I make translation faster?

In the QuickTranslate app, under Fast languages, click Download a Language… and download the languages you read most. Pages in those languages then translate almost instantly, even offline, because QuickTranslate prepares the translation in the background before you click. Without a fast language, translation still works, but it's much slower.

To remove a downloaded language: System Settings › General › Language & Region › Translation Languages.

Why not Safari's own translate button?

I built QuickTranslate because Safari's button doesn't work the way I wanted, in three ways:

  • It doesn't always appear. Safari often doesn't offer to translate a page, especially government websites. QuickTranslate's button is always there, and it detects the page's language by itself.
  • It reloads the page. If you've started filling in a form, it's gone. QuickTranslate changes only the text, so what you typed stays.
  • It takes more than one click. A menu opens first. QuickTranslate is one click, or ⌥⇧T.
A page says it can't be translated.

Apple's translation doesn't support every language pair yet. If the page's language is supported and it still fails, please email me the page's address.

Some text on the page isn't translated.

QuickTranslate translates the page's text. Text inside images, inside embedded frames (like some ads and videos), and the hints in empty form fields stay as they are. A sentence split up by a link or bold text is translated in parts, so it can read less well. If a page doesn't translate well, I'd like to know: email me its address.

Does it send the pages I read anywhere?

No. Translation happens on your Mac with Apple's built-in translation. See the privacy policy.
