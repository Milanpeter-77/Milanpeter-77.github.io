OneClickTranslate

A Safari extension for the Mac that translates web pages with one click, on your Mac. OneClickTranslate is in beta: it works, and it's still getting better — your feedback helps a lot.

How do I turn on the extension?

Open OneClickTranslate once, then in Safari choose Safari › Settings › Extensions and tick OneClickTranslate. Allow it on the websites you want to translate. Its button then appears in Safari's toolbar.

How do I choose the language pages are translated into?

Open the OneClickTranslate app and pick your language. The extension uses it right away.

The first translation of a language is slow.

The first time you translate from a new language, macOS may need to download that language from Apple. After that it works offline and much faster. You can manage downloaded languages in System Settings › General › Language & Region › Translation Languages.

A page says it can't be translated.

Apple's translation doesn't support every language pair yet. If the page's language is supported and it still fails, please email me the page's address.

Does it send the pages I read anywhere?

No. Translation happens on your Mac with Apple's built-in translation. See the privacy policy.
