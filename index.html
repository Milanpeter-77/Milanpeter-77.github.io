Sheet 03 — About

About

Hi, I'm Milan. I'm from Hungary, and I live in the Netherlands.

My apps start the same way: there's something I want to do on my Mac or my iPhone, so I go looking for an app. I try a few, and none of them is quite right — not good enough for my standards. So I decided: if I need it, I build it.

But I don't want to keep them to myself. Each app here began as a fix for my own problem, and now it's yours too. I design, build and support every one of them, so if something doesn't work the way you expect, you'll be talking to the person who made it.
