Sheet 03 — About

About

Hi, I'm Milan Peter. I'm from Hungary, and I live in the Netherlands.

I'm a creative thinker at heart. I love designing things and watching an idea in my head turn into something real. Apps are where I get to live that out: I build each one the way I imagine it, down to the small details.

Most of them start with a need of my own. There's something I want to do on my Mac or my iPhone, so I look for an app, try a few, and none is quite right — not good enough for my standards. So I decided: if I need it, I build it.

And if it's useful to me, it might be useful to you too. So I don't keep my apps to myself — I share them. I design, build and support every one, so if something doesn't work the way you expect, you'll be talking to the person who made it.

QuickTranslate

One-click page translation, in Safari.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store

QuickTranslate puts a translate button in Safari's toolbar that's always there and always does one thing: translate the page you're on, right away.

One click

Click the button, or press ⌥⇧T, and the page is translated in place. Click again and the original is back.

No reload

Only the text on the page changes. A form you've started filling in keeps everything you typed.

Works on every page

It detects each page's language by itself, so it also works on pages that don't say which language they're in, like many government websites.

The language at a glance

The toolbar button shows the page's language, three dots while it translates, and your language when it's done.

Ready before you click

Download the languages you read most, and their pages translate almost instantly, even offline. New text that appears while you read is translated too.

Private by design

Translation happens on your Mac with Apple's built-in translation. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Why I built it

Safari has its own translate button, but it often doesn't appear (government websites especially), it reloads the page, so a half-filled form is lost, and it opens a menu first. QuickTranslate fixes exactly these three things, and nothing more.

  • RequiresmacOS 26.4 or later · Safari
  • LanguagesEvery language Apple can translate
  • Data collectedNone

Widgets+

The widgets Apple's apps are missing.

In development

Widgets+ adds more useful widgets for the apps that came with your iPhone — Calendar, Clock, Contacts, Fitness, Health, Maps, Reminders, Sleep, Weather and more — made to look exactly like Apple's own.

Home Screen

Widgets in every size, in Light, Dark or Liquid Glass, with the same fonts, colors and layouts as Apple's.

Lock Screen

Circular, Rectangular and Inline widgets, made for a glance at the Lock Screen.

Settings for every widget

Each widget and size has its own settings: choose what it shows, how and for which calendar, list or place.

Launchers

Your favorite apps in one widget, as icons or tiles.

Apple Watch

Complications for the watch face, in color or tinted. Being built now.

Your data stays yours

Your calendars, health and contacts are read on your iPhone and never sent anywhere.
